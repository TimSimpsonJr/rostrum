# House notes: what has held across what the owner has reviewed

Observations from the owner's reviews of earlier films, each with where it came from. They are notes, not a
checklist: nothing fails a build for departing from one, and the narration and the user's instructions come first.
They are here so a choice is made knowing what has and has not gone down well before.

- **Nothing freezes.** Everything animates on ones: each frame differs from the one before, and a hold is a slow
  drift. ("A static frame longer than half a second is a bug"; "don't animate on different frame rates." How-to
  and explainer reviews; a window held still in the showcase register is deliberate: `references/showcase.md`.)
- **Nothing pops in.** What appears is uncovered, drawn or set in place over several frames. ("Checkboxes should
  animate on, not just appear.")
- **No cross-dissolve between two screens of text.** Two layers of text seen through each other read as a mistake;
  a cut, or a blur under the incoming type, has gone down well. (First tutorial.)
- **Nothing readable is sliced by the frame's edge.** (First tutorial: a menu item cut by the frame's right edge.)
- **A logo is the brand's file as supplied:** not redrawn, recoloured, or given a tagline or a parent-company line
  that is not in the file. ("No parent-company line until a proper lockup exists.")
- **No ornament:** pill tags with a dot, small "eyebrow" labels above headings, badges, monospace capitals. ("Looks
  super AI.") Plain short labels that name what a thing is have been welcome.
- **Accurate to the site without chasing pixels** ("the abstraction level hits the sweet spot"), **and no
  interface states the walkthrough did not show** ("prefer the page without focus rings").
- **Sounds are many and quiet:** small tactile sounds, one per visible event, a click for interface moments; not
  music-bed synth. (Explainer reviews.)
- **Things travel on smooth curves, not right angles,** and overlap rather than arrive one by one. ("The boxes
  should already be moving.")
- **Stand-ins are labels, not look-alikes:** "Transaction 1", round amounts in order, fills that are plausible and
  plainly fake. (First tutorial; the tools are in `scrub.md`.)
